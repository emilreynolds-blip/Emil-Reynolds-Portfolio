CASUALTY EXTRACTION · INVENTOR

RSCU Rescue Litter

RSCU stands for Rapid, Semi rigid, Cross functional, Ultralight. The packable litter supports casualty extraction by hand carry, drag, or hands free tow, with modular use of its body and straps. Product literature also describes unmanned ground vehicle extraction.

Work Completed

Emil Reynolds is the named inventor on Rescue litter, US 11,925,583 B2, granted March 12, 2024. The invention describes a stretcher sheet with guided support straps. Published product materials describe modular carrying and towing, internal radial support webbing, and a compact roll up configuration. Combat Bound received a 2023 USAF Phase I SBIR award of $62,747 for RSCU (contract FA8649-23-P-0135). The award supports the research record; it is not a claim of operational certification.
