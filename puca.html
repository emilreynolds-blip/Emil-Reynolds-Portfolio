EW / SIGINT · CO INVENTOR

Puca

A compact, software defined, networked electronic warfare concept for individuals, small teams, vehicles, and unmanned platforms. Emil Reynolds is a named co inventor with Raul Washington John Donoso, Jr. on the Portable electromagnetic countermeasure device patent, US 11,973,585 B2, granted April 30, 2024.

Work Completed

Work completed: Original Puca v0.6.0 includes a hardware neutral observation contract, synthetic passive replay, bounded SigMF offline analysis, power rise alerts, an offline dashboard, signed credential verification, and replay/clock integrity checks. The recovered development record reports 35 local tests passing. Live SDR integration, calibrated direction finding, operational effects, and field performance are not established by that prototype. Combat Bound also received a 2023 USAF Phase I SBIR award for Puca; that company award is distinct from prototype validation.
